🤖 WBK Weber Robot:
Christmas Telecom Quest

A quick 2D platformer prototype inspired by classic side-scrollers. Run, jump, collect spectrum snowflakes, dodge interference bots, and restore holiday broadband across 10 telecom-themed Christmas levels.

Controls: Arrow keys / A-D to move, Space / W / Up to jump. On mobile, use the buttons.
